Token Radar (radar-7) ranks all 758 protocols in a frozen DefiLlama snapshot (2026-11-14) by an implied value: TVL times the median market-cap/TVL multiple of live-token protocols in the same category, adjusted for 7-day growth, track-record age and chain breadth. The census is complete: 178 clean pre-token launch candidates (all 40 watchlist protocols plus 138 smaller early-discovery protocols below the $50M floor), 164 flagged pre-token rows, and 416 protocols whose token already trades. Every row carries a census rank; the clean pre-token rows also carry a launch rank, and the top launch candidate is rank 1. Each pre-token row gets a 7-step farm plan with net dollar economics, a token-launch probability from a fitted odds model, and a likely rank range from the comparables' own spread. Live rows show the market's own answer beside the model's: actual market cap and the model-versus-market ratio. The dashboard is a sortable, filterable board with a detail view per protocol, the full category-multiple table, a held-out validation table, the odds-model coefficients, and a coverage ledger accounting for all 758 protocols. ## What radar-7 adds

- **The whole census, 178 → 758 rows.** Every protocol in the snapshot is ranked by model-implied value. Rows carry a status: clean pre-token (178), flagged pre-token (164: 20 above the $50M floor and off the watchlist, 11 versioned names, 81 wrapped / staked / bridged products, 52 exchange products), or already trading (416: 119 with a market cap, 269 recording no market cap, 14 recording a dead market cap, 14 exchanges). Flagged and live rows carry no launch rank — the flag text says they are not standalone launches — but they keep their census rank, so nothing is hidden and the ledger reconciles: 178 + 164 + 416 = 758.
- **Net farm economics, in dollars.** The deposit is deployed capital and comes back on exit, so the only sunk cash cost is the gas, and the farm breaks even when the eventual airdrop is worth the gas. The plan also states a pro-rata scenario: if the airdrop were shared out purely by TVL at the protocol's implied value, the farmer's share would be the deposit × multiple × growth × maturity × breadth, and the scenario net and dollars per hour follow from that share minus the gas. Real airdrops are rarely pro-rata, so the scenario is a yardstick for comparing farms, not a promised payout — and the airdrop itself is never priced, because it cannot be.
- **Token odds.** A logistic regression fitted on the snapshot's own launched-versus-pre-token split (416 launched, 342 pre-token), from log TVL, age, chain count, 7-day change and the live share of the protocol's category (leave-one-out). Cross-validated AUC is 0.683 — better than chance, far from certain — and every pre-token row shows its probability plus which features lift it and which pull it down.
- **Held-out validation of the value terms.** Five-fold cross-validation over the 119 live-token comparables, with each fold's multiples computed from the other four folds only, so a held-out protocol never prices itself. The full model scores a median absolute percentage error of 92.2% and a mean error of 39.7%, with 61.3% of predictions within 2× of the true market cap. The multiples spread is wide, so implied values are order-of-magnitude ranks, not prices — and the board says so. An ablation switches each value term off in turn: the terms barely move the median error, but they cut the mean error from 55.2% (multiple only) to 39.7% (full model), so they tame the worst misses and stay in.

## Decisions the brief left open

- **Two ranks.** Launch rank is the rank among the 178 clean pre-token protocols; census rank is the position among all 758. A clean pre-token row shows both; flagged and live rows show only the census rank.
- **Live rows are valued the same way.** A live row's implied value uses the same formula as a pre-token row, so the model-versus-market ratio compares like with like. Its own market cap sits inside its category median, which the median tolerates; the held-out validation prices the same effect properly, with each fold's multiples built without the held-out protocols.
- **Versioned names.** A name is versioned when it ends in a space followed by a version marker: V/v plus digits (V2, v3, V12, V2.0) or a bare numeric version (2.0, 3.0). So "Rysk V12" is versioned and flagged, while "CommunityV3" (no space before V3) and "Project 0" (a bare 0 is not a version marker) stay clean.
- **Exchanges first.** The 14 exchanges are set aside from the trading protocols before the comparables are counted, so the 119 comparables are live-token protocols with a positive market cap that are not exchanges; the ledger's 269 / 14 / 14 split follows from that order.
- **Quartiles.** The 25th and 75th percentiles use linear interpolation on the sorted multiples (the same method as the median), and each quartile multiple is clamped to 0.05–1.50 like the median. A quartile value's launch rank is 1 + the number of clean pre-token rows with a strictly greater implied value.
- **Drivers.** Each row's three drivers are the factors with the largest absolute log-distance from their neutral value: TVL and the multiple against the board medians, the three multipliers against 1.
- **Farm totals.** Each step states its own gas cost and time; the farm total sums them ($3 setup + $2 first action + $2 per weekly repeat + $2 second feature) over W weeks, where W = round(hold days / 7). The position capital ($50–$250 by TVL tier) is deployed capital, shown separately as recoverable on exit.
- **"Records no token".** The board only ever claims a protocol records no token in the snapshot; the record is not proof that no token exists. That label noise is in the odds model's training data too, and the odds inherit it.

How the value is worked out

Implied value = max(1, round(TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth))

  • Category multiple — median Mcap/TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 needed); otherwise the global median across all 119 live-token protocols. Clamped to 0.05–1.50.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day change, −60, 120) / 100 × 0.5. A missing 7-day change counts as 0.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4. Age is the snapshot date minus the day DefiLlama first tracked the protocol; unknown counts as 365 days.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.04 × min(chains − 1, 5).
  • Launch rank — the rank among the 178 clean pre-token protocols by implied value. Census rank — the position among all 758 protocols. Flagged and already-trading rows carry no launch rank.
  • Likely rank A–B — the same calculation with the category's 25th and 75th percentile multiples instead of the median, showing the launch ranks those values would take. It is the comparables' own spread, not a guess.
  • Token odds — a logistic regression fitted on the snapshot's own launched-versus-pre-token split (416 launched, 342 pre-token), from log TVL, age, chain count, 7-day change and the live share of the protocol's category. It is a probability, not a promise, and it is only shown for pre-token rows.
  • Farm economics — the deposit is deployed capital and comes back on exit, so the only sunk cash cost is the gas, and the farm breaks even when the eventual airdrop is worth the gas. The scenario dollars per hour ask what the farm would pay if the airdrop were shared out purely pro-rata by TVL at implied value; real airdrops are rarely pro-rata, so treat it as a yardstick for comparing farms, not a payout.

Held-out validation — the value terms tested on data they never saw

Five-fold cross-validation over the 119 live-token comparables: each fold's multiples are computed from the other four folds only, so a held-out protocol never prices itself. Median absolute percentage error (MdAPE) is the headline number; the mean is dominated by the worst misses.

Flagged rows are ranked in the census but carry no launch rank: above the floor and off the watchlist, a pre-token-looking protocol is most likely a chain deployment or product of a protocol whose token already trades, and the snapshot carries no parent field to tell them apart — so they are leads to verify, not confirmed launches.

"Records no token" means the snapshot lists no token for the protocol; the record is not proof that no token exists.

Live rows show the market's own answer beside the model's: the actual market cap and the model-versus-market ratio. Where the model says much more than the market, the multiple is probably too generous for that category; where it says much less, the market may be pricing something the snapshot cannot see.
